A curious mind in biology

I am Renuka Sri G, a first-year B.Sc Biotechnology student at Vivekanandha College of Arts and Science for Women, with a strong interest in Biotechnology, Bioinformatics, and scientific research.

As I begin my academic journey, I am focused on developing a strong understanding of biological sciences while exploring modern biotechnology and computational approaches.

I am particularly interested in understanding how biotechnology and bioinformatics can work together to solve real-world problems in healthcare, life sciences, agriculture, and scientific research.

My goal is to continuously learn, develop practical and computational skills, gain real-world experience, and eventually build a career as a Biotechnologist and Research Scientist.

Why Biotechnology?

Science can change life.

Biotechnology combines biology, science, technology, and innovation to address challenges across healthcare, agriculture, food, industry, and the environment.

I am interested in Biotechnology because it provides an opportunity to understand living systems while exploring how scientific knowledge can be transformed into practical solutions.

The growing connection between Biotechnology and Bioinformatics especially interests me because computational approaches are becoming increasingly important in modern biological research.
